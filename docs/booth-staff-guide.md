# Booth staff guide

For anyone helping at the booth. No technical knowledge needed: this is what
is going on, and what to tell people who want to enter.

## What's going on

We're giving away books. Anyone at the conference can enter, for free, on
their own phone. At set times we draw winners at random.

- **Printed books** are handed over at the booth, so the winner has to be
  there when their name is called.
- **Ebooks and other digital prizes** are emailed to the winner, so they
  don't need to be there.

## How someone enters

1. Point them to the **flyer**. They open their phone's camera and point it
   at the QR code (the square pattern). A link appears; they tap it.
   - QR code not working? They can type the short address printed under it
     into their phone's browser, such as `drawing.example.com/c/pycon-us-2027/`.
2. They fill in their **name** and **email address**.
3. A few questions follow. They're optional; people can skip them.
4. They tick the box that says they'll get one follow-up email. (This one is
   required.) A second box, to subscribe to a newsletter, is optional and
   unticked.
5. They tap **Enter the drawing**. The page says **"Thanks — you're in the
   drawing."** and lists the drawing times. That's it.

## What to tell people

- **"One entry covers every drawing."** They don't need to enter again for
  each one. Entering again with the same email doesn't add chances; it just
  shows the same thanks page.
- **"Enter before the drawing starts."** A drawing only includes people who
  entered before it began. Entering later still counts for the drawings that
  come after.
- **"Be here at drawing time to win a printed book."** The times are on the
  flyer and on the thanks page. If a printed book's winner isn't here, we
  draw someone else on the spot.
- **"Missed your name? You're still in."** Someone who wasn't here when
  called can still win a later drawing.
- **"You can win once."** Each person wins at most one prize per conference,
  so more people get a chance.
- **"Ebook winners get an email."** No need to stay for those.

## During a drawing

The organizer starts the drawing on their phone, and the winners' names
appear there (and on the booth screen, if there is one). For a printed book:

1. Call the name out.
2. If the person comes forward, hand over the book. The organizer marks it
   **Claimed**.
3. If nobody comes forward after a reasonable wait, the organizer taps **Not
   here**, and a new name is drawn right away.

## Common questions

**"Will you spam me?"**
No. Entering means one follow-up email after the conference. The newsletter
is only for people who ticked that box.

**"What do you do with my information?"**
The entry form has a link, "How your information is used", that explains it.

**"Can I enter for my friend?"**
Best if the friend enters on their own phone: entering means agreeing to the
follow-up email, and that should be their choice. A printed book goes only to
the person whose name is called, if they're here.

**"I entered twice by mistake."**
No problem: the second entry is ignored.

**"The page says 'Too many tries'."**
Too many entries came from one place (such as the conference Wi-Fi) in a
minute. Wait a minute and try again.

**"The page says entries are closed."**
That conference's drawings are over.

**"Can I (a booth helper) enter?"**
The organizer puts staff email addresses on a list that is left out of the
drawings. If yours is on it, you can try the form, but you can't win.

**Anything else**, or a page that looks broken: ask the organizer.
